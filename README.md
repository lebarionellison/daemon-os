# Daemon OS

## Infrastructure Intelligence for Modern Systems

**Your infrastructure is alive. Give it intelligence.**

Daemon OS is an AI-native infrastructure intelligence platform for infrastructure telemetry, security, operational visibility, AI-assisted operations, and predictive failure analysis.

**Live Website:** https://lebarionellison.github.io/daemon-os/

---

## What Daemon OS Does

Daemon OS brings infrastructure telemetry, intelligence, security, and operations into one command center.

### Living Infrastructure Graph
Understand relationships between services, systems, dependencies, and infrastructure components.

### Failure Forecast
Identify signals that may indicate an upcoming infrastructure failure before it becomes an incident.

### Blast Radius
Understand what systems, services, and workloads could be affected when something goes wrong.

### AI Incident Replay
Analyze operational events and reconstruct what happened to help teams understand and respond faster.

### Daemon Autopilot
Turn infrastructure signals into intelligent operational recommendations and automated workflows.

### GPU Intelligence
Monitor and understand GPU infrastructure for modern AI and machine-learning workloads.

## Why Daemon OS?

Traditional monitoring tells you **what is happening**.

Daemon OS is designed to help you understand:

**What is happening ? Why it matters ? What could fail ? What will be affected ? What to do next**

The goal is to move infrastructure operations from reactive monitoring toward intelligent operations.
